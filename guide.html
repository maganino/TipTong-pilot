TipTong, on your devices

The app on your phone, the extension in Chrome — each takes under a minute.

Step 0 — open TipTong

Scan the QR code with your phone camera, or type the address into your browser:

maganino.github.io/TipTong-pilot

iPhone / iPad

in Safari
  1. 1

    Open the link above in Safari (the QR code opens it there automatically).

  2. 2

    Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.

  3. 3

    Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

in Chrome
  1. 1

    Open the link above in Chrome.

  2. 2

    Tap Install app when Chrome offers it — or open the ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen.

  3. 3

    Confirm with Install.

Computer — the Chrome extension

in Chrome on your laptop/desktop · turns any web page or PDF into exercises
  1. 1

    Download tiptong-extension.zip — this link always gives you the latest version. Double-click the file to unzip it; you get a folder with the same name.

  2. 2

    In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter. Switch on Developer mode (toggle in the top-right corner).

  3. 3

    Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder. TipTong appears in the list — done.

  4. 4

    Click the 🧩 puzzle icon next to the address bar and pin TipTong so it's always one click away.

  5. 5

    The first time you open TipTong on a page, a short guide walks you through the four steps — choose a mode, highlight, hide, add a cue. Reopen it anytime with the ? button in the TipTong toolbar.

Updating: when we announce a new version, download the zip again, unzip it over the old folder, and press the ↻ reload arrow on TipTong's card in chrome://extensions.
Chrome may remind you at startup that a developer-mode extension is running — that's expected during the pilot. Once TipTong is on the Chrome Web Store, it will install by link and update itself.

First time: log in once

Open TipTong from your home screen and log in with the email and password you were invited with. You only do this once. No account yet? Request a demo at mina@tiptong.eu and we'll set one up for you.

Works offline

After that first visit, TipTong opens and plays even without internet — on the train, on a plane, anywhere. Your exercises live on your phone.
